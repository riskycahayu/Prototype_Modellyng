ABOUT MODELYNG

Transforming Academic Papers into
Structured Knowledge Representations

Modellyng is an AI-powered academic knowledge modeling platform designed to help students, lecturers, and researchers analyze and synthesize academic literature in a more structured, efficient, and transparent way.

Unlike AI tools that focus primarily on summarization, Modellyng aims to transform scientific papers into structured knowledge representations, enabling users to compare research findings, explore relationships between publications, and identify potential research gaps.

Through source traceability and a human-in-the-loop approach, Modellyng emphasizes evidence verification, transparency, and responsible AI use in academic research.

The Friction in Academia

The Problem We Are Solving

The rapid surge in global publications creates cognitive overload and fragmented evidence tracking for researchers.

Increasing Academic Publications

Millions of papers published annually make comprehensive literature reviews daunting and exhausting to keep up with.

Time-Consuming Literature Review

Researchers spend hundreds of manual hours extracting methodologies, sampling criteria, and underlying theoretical claims.

Difficult Cross-Paper Comparison

Manually mapping varied terminologies and disparate experiment setups across dozens of PDF papers often causes blind spots.

Limited Research Gap Visibility

Identifying unexplored research niches remains intuitive rather than systematic, leaving high-potential gaps unaddressed.
